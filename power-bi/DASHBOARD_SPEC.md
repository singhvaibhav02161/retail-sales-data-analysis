# Power BI Dashboard Specification

## Page 1 — Executive Overview
KPI cards: Revenue, Profit, Orders, AOV, Profit Margin.

Visuals:
- Monthly revenue line chart
- Revenue by region bar chart
- Category contribution chart

## Page 2 — Regional Performance
- Region slicer
- Revenue vs Profit by region
- Orders by region
- Profit margin by region

## Page 3 — Product & Category
- Top 10 products by revenue
- Revenue by category
- Profit by category
- Product-level drill-down

## Page 4 — Trends
- Monthly revenue and profit
- Month-over-month comparison
- Date and region slicers

## UX Guidelines
Keep the dashboard single-purpose, use consistent KPI formatting, expose slicers clearly, and avoid unnecessary decorative visuals.
